## 🔬 Research

**🎥 TCSG-CAR: Privacy-Preserving Multi-Camera Scene Understanding** · *Final Year Project*<br/>
Follows people across non-overlapping cameras and explains events in natural language, **without face recognition or biometrics**, using temporal scene graphs, a 3D knowledge graph and causal RAG. I lead the causal retrieval and fuzzy reasoning components.<br/>
📄 *TCSG: Face-Independent Cross-Camera Correspondence with Structured Visual Timelines and Multiplex Temporal Graphs*, under review at **IEEE ICRA 2027**

**🐕 Socially Aware Navigation & Gesture Interaction for a Quadruped Robot** · *with WSO2 Lanka*<br/>
Helping a Unitree Go2 robot recognise its operator in a crowd and move safely around people. I built the operator re-identification module (YOLOv8 + OSNet) and lead the Interval Type-2 fuzzy logic navigation work.<br/>
📄 Two papers in preparation for **ICAISC 2027**

<sub>Code is private while papers are under review. Happy to share details on request.</sub>
